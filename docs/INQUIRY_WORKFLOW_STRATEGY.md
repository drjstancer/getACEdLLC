# Institutional Inquiry Workflow Strategy

## Purpose
The inquiry experience should feel calm, professional, intentional, and institutionally credible.

The goal is not aggressive conversion.
The goal is trust-centered engagement.

---

## Primary Inquiry Categories
### Speaking Engagements
- keynote presentations
- workshops
- conferences
- leadership events
- educational initiatives

### Institutional Consulting
- strategy development
- leadership cultivation
- educational pathway design
- institutional culture work
- mentorship ecosystem development

### Adult Mental Health First Aid Training
- institutional training requests
- educational wellness initiatives
- staff and leadership training

### Partnerships & Collaborations
- pathway partnerships
- educational collaborations
- leadership initiatives
- organizational partnerships

---

## Experience Principles
The inquiry process should feel:
- thoughtful
- easy
- structured
- warm
- institutionally mature
- editorially refined

Avoid:
- sales funnel language
- aggressive marketing tactics
- cluttered forms
- overwhelming workflows

---

## Long-Term Direction
The inquiry infrastructure should eventually support:
- automated acknowledgements
- categorized intake routing
- scheduling integration
- institutional onboarding
- partnership workflow management
