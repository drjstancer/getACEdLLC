# Editorial Content Architecture

## Purpose
The insights ecosystem exists to establish get ACEd, LLC as a respected voice in transformational educational leadership, mentorship, institutional culture, and community-centered excellence.

---

## Editorial Structure
### Featured Essays
Long-form flagship thought leadership pieces exploring transformational educational leadership and institutional culture.

### Leadership Reflections
Shorter reflections connected to educational systems, mentorship, leadership, and care-centered excellence.

### Frameworks & Models
Research-informed practical frameworks supporting institutional transformation and student success.

### Speaking Expansions
Expanded written reflections connected to keynote presentations, workshops, and institutional engagements.

---

## Reading Experience Philosophy
The editorial experience should feel:
- spacious
- thoughtful
- cinematic
- intellectually grounded
- emotionally intelligent
- easy to read

Avoid:
- cluttered blog aesthetics
- excessive sidebars
- aggressive calls-to-action
- SEO-first writing structures

---

## Long-Term Vision
The platform should evolve into a respected educational leadership publication ecosystem centered on transformational leadership, mentorship, institutional culture, and community-rooted excellence.
